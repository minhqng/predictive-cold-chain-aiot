# Hazard ledger v0 (W01)

**Scope:** W01 design hazards for the ColdGuard P0 cooling-control path.
W01 itself contains no policy implementation or automated test evidence.
**Status:** v0 living ledger. Not an FMEA, not a certification, no quantitative
risk acceptance.
**Owners:** Doc 04 ownership; "D" means Member D, but detection, hardware
characterization and cloud controls remain with A/B/C/W11/W13 as named.

Severity is intentionally qualitative (`S1` high / `S2` medium / `S3` low) until
the team freezes a risk method. `Open` means no complete, verified mitigation
exists in this workspace. Test IDs in the Evidence column are planned W03 or
later verification targets; they are not present or executed in this W01 branch.

## Explicitly out of scope for this ledger version

- Quantitative severity/probability, detectability scoring and residual-risk
  acceptance. These require a team-agreed risk method and field evidence.
- Cybersecurity threat modeling beyond the P0 baseline; see
  `security-baseline-v0.md`.
- Physical fault injection (no device was driven, no heater/fan was forced).
- Multi-chamber/site-level hazards, maintenance mistakes and supply-chain
  compromise. These are not covered by Docs 00–05D evidence available here.

## Change rule

A hazard row may move to `Mitigated` only with a reproducible test/evidence
reference and the named owner's confirmation. Until then it stays `Partial` or
`Open`; a design note is not a verified mitigation.
