The committed capture contains no door-open event, and the door mapping is a switch whose two settings assign opposite states to every raw level (`docs/01` §8.1/§8.2 forbid assuming either polarity without physical verification), so the protocol below is what has to be executed, not what was observed. ## 2. Planned durations and repetitions

* run-id prefix `CG-S2-A-`, repetitions 6, planned durations [15, 30, 60] s, horizon 900 s at 1 Hz, unchanged from the shipped telemetry

## 3. Per-repetition procedure

1. confirm supervised attendance: the operator is at the bench for the whole repetition, with the stop-on-abnormal-current rule restated before ARM
2. boot the board, open the capture BEFORE ARM, record boot_id
3. operator attests the MC-38 mapping for THIS boot with `DOOR NEAR_LOW <token>`, then confirms the lid is seated and reads door_open=0 (CLOSED)
4. hold a >=120 s CLOSED baseline at the target controller state
5. open the door fully for the planned duration, starting the operator timer at the same instant the lid breaks the seal
6. close the lid at the planned duration, keeping the timer reading
7. record recovery until all chamber-air probes return within the pairing band or the stage deadline
8. stop the capture, then write: planned duration, measured timer duration, operator, initial state, anomalies

## 4. MC-38 timing method (A-W10-02)

* machine ground truth: the MC-38 transition (50 ms debounce on a 10 ms poll), counted by `door_cycles` on the frame stream
* the frame stream is 1.0 Hz, so the resolved edge time is +/-1.0 s and the minimum resolvable open duration is 1.0 s - the operator timer is the only sub-second check and stays secondary
* cross-check rule: sum of detected OPEN->CLOSED transitions must equal the device's final door_cycles; a mismatch means the capture missed an edge and the pilot run is not usable
* machine counter field: `door_cycles`

## 5. Per-boot polarity attestation

`DOOR NEAR_LOW|NEAR_HIGH <boot_token>` names a level; it does not prove a physical door position, and the confirmation is per boot. `detect_events` therefore reports every unattested segment as untrusted and never folds `UNKNOWN` into `CLOSED`.
