## The ask, and what it can honestly become

docs/05A A-W14-01 says *verify no movement since freeze*. Archived telemetry cannot verify physical non-movement: the frames carry no sensor ROM ids, so channel identity across boots is not established by the wire, and no frame records mounting, tip height, clearance, orientation or cable path. What this deliverable is therefore written as: a **method plus a baseline**, so the next capture can be checked, with the two states kept apart instead of blurred.

The sentence this tool refuses to print is `"no movement verified"`; the guard in `cg_w14_sensor_qa.guard_no_forbidden_claim` and this dossier's validator both fail if it ever appears as an assertion.

## What each mismatch would mean, physically

A flag nobody can interpret is not a QA method, so every check names its own consequences. These are stated as candidates for inspection, not conclusions.

- **`capture_role_label_mismatch`** - the firmware's role column no longer matches the label the frozen record documents, which means the image, pin-map version or slot-to-channel map changed - the capture is then describing a different logical geometry, not the frozen one. It is not by itself proof that a probe was moved.
- **`hot_side_channel_moved`** - the channel that rises while the bridge is driven is no longer the slot the record calls TEC hot side: either the hot-side probe left the stack, an air probe is now touching the stack or a rack, or the 1-Wire slots were re-mapped at the connector. Any of these invalidates the air/hot split every W07 statistic is built on.
- **`air_triad_direction_change`** - one of the three chamber-air channels stopped falling while driven, so that tip is no longer sampling the air volume the other two agree about - the most likely causes are a probe that moved, became coupled to a surface, or became electrically stale while the value held.
- **`air_separation_change`** - the mutual spread of the three air probes left the band the baseline session shows. Because that band comes from one run's within-session drift, this is a flag to look, not a measurement of movement: it also fires if the chamber started at a different load or the door was left cracked.
- **`level_offset_beyond_baseline_band`** - the absolute levels sit outside the baseline session's own drift band. That is expected whenever start conditions differ, so on its own it says nothing about position; it only matters together with a direction or separation change.
- **`rank_order_change`** - the ordering of the channels by level flipped. Positions in a stratified chamber set the ordering, so a persistent flip with everything else unchanged is the pattern a probe swap or a probe that moved a shelf over would leave.
