## 5. Measurement plan

### Duty point set

* method: start from the points the plant already has data for, then fill the gaps: committed 999 permille and the H6 100 permille point exist in git; the 200-900 ladder exists only in the local bench tree and in two different wiring regimes, so it is not one ladder
* why: docs/05A says 'test limited safe duty points' - limited means the smallest set that spans the regime, and 'safe' is decided after P1-P9

### Repetition

* method: at least three dwells per duty point, randomized order, with a rest interval long enough for the hot side to return to its starting window; the rest interval is itself a parameter to be recorded, not assumed
* why: the existing ladder is one 60 s dwell per point, which cannot separate duty effect from start-up transient

### Dwell length

* method: long enough for the hot-side plateau criterion (drift per minute below a stated floor) rather than a fixed 60 s; the plateau rule already used in cg_w08_hot_side.py is the shape of such a criterion
* why: a 60 s dwell never reached a thermal steady state at 100 % duty (H8-A was still warming at 1.46 K/min after 178 s)

### Per-frame exclusions

* method: drop and list the first frame of every driven block and the first two frames after the applied duty reaches zero (the documented register latency), keep every excluded frame in the artifact
* why: one duty-0 frame at 7.15 A otherwise enters an OFF average as if it were a measurement

### Recorded fields per dwell

* method: pwm_req/applied/readback, voltage/current/power/energy with their status and age tokens, T1/T2/T3/T4 with roles, door, fan, fault_bits, stop_reason, off_verify, boot_id, run_id, and the touch-free component temperatures at fixed checkpoints
* why: the ablation feature set and the safety argument need the same frame, and the age tokens are what makes the values usable later

### Failure criteria (all named parameters, none invented)

* method: collapse of current at a commanded nonzero duty; any frame above the resolved hot-side limit; off-current gate failure at stop; any fault_bits nonzero; any component above its stated rating; energy counter inconsistent with the power integral beyond its quantum
* why: a NO-GO must be provable from the capture, not argued from a feeling

### Decision rule

* method: GO only if every precondition holds, every criterion's parameter has a resolved value with cited provenance, and the collected dwells satisfy those parameters. Otherwise NO-GO (the evidence says stop) or NOT_DECIDED (the parameters do not exist yet).
* why: these are different outcomes and must not be merged

## 6. What the existing captures already say about partial duty

They say less than a characterization needs, and the honest list is short:

* one committed 100 per mille point at 60 s (H6, 0.96 A median, 11.2 W
  median) and one committed 999 per mille point at 600 s (H8-C, 7.16 A
  median, 84.4 W median, 9.99 A peak) - **the only two duty levels with
  electrical data inside git**;
* a 200-1000 per mille ladder in Member A's local bench tree, one 60 s dwell
  per point, split across two wiring regimes (20-60 % through a degraded
  return path at ~0.268 ohm, 70-100 % after its replacement at ~0.019-0.021
  ohm): 600 per mille drew 4.33 A before and 5.41 A after the fix, so the two
  halves are not one curve and were never pooled here;
* duty readback matched applied in every recorded frame (600/600 driven,
  357/357 idle) - the one part of the IBT-2 response that is actually
  observable;
* no repeated dwell at any duty level, no randomized order, no component
  temperatures, no fan observation, and a PZEM counter too coarse (1 Wh) to
  resolve short dwells.

Consequence: the electrical-versus-duty relation in the local ladder looks
smooth and monotone, which is a reason to *expect* the characterization to be
informative, and not a result.
