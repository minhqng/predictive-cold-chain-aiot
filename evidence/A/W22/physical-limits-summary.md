**Status:** **PUBLISHED_EVIDENCE**

## 1. Physical Limitations

- **Scale & Quantity:** 1 benchtop thermal prototype unit. No cross-unit manufacturing variance observed.
- **TEC / Module Uncertainty:** Single TEC1-12706 module with uncharacterized Seebeck thermal backflow when unpowered.
- **Sensor Limitations:** DS18B20 1-Wire bus has finite conversion time (~750 ms at 12-bit resolution) and thermal inertia.
- **Ambient Coupling:** Test chamber is non-hermetic styrofoam box susceptible to external ambient fluctuations.
- **PZEM Response:** PZEM-017 current updates via Modbus RTU at ~1 Hz with internal averaging filter lag.
