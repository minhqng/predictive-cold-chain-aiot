## 1. Post-Campaign Inspection Checklist

1. **Sensor Identity Re-check:** Read all 1-Wire ROMs on the bus; verify no missing sensors or address drops.
2. **Mechanical & Thermal Audit:** Verify heatsink mounting screws remain torqued; check fan bearings and airflow obstruction.
3. **Electrical Connections:** Inspect power supply terminal block, IBT-2 solder joints, and PZEM screw terminals for loosening or thermal discoloration.
4. **Physical Config Recording:** Record final `hardware_config_id` and document any exceptions or drift.
