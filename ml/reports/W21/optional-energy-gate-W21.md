## Why the optional scope is closed

- `NO_AUTHORITATIVE_AUTHORIZATION_ARTIFACT`
- `CORE_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`
- `OPTIONAL_SCOPE_CANCELLED_OR_NOT_OPENED`

The canonical condition is an authorized optional scope after core evidence is complete (Doc 03 Gate F; Doc 04 W21 optional opening; Doc 05B B-W21-04). No authoritative authorization artifact bound to bytes exists, and core evidence is incomplete.
