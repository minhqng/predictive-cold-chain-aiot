## 4. Ceiling effect and what the result does not mean

The thermal/context arm alone already reaches AP 1.0000 in every fold. The W11
fixture's S2 thermal trajectory is generated by the prescribed door event, and the
labels are generated by the same fixture; thermal features therefore identify the
scenario directly. A3b cannot improve on a ceiling.

Model inspection of the refit A3b artifact exposes the shortcut more specifically:
the 60-tree booster uses exactly one feature, `time_since_open` (60 of 60 splits,
threshold 60 s); all 27 other features, including every electrical feature, are
unused. The final artifact is therefore a door-time rule induced by the synthetic
fixture. This is a native split-usage diagnostic, not causal importance and not
evidence about a real plant. The A3a arm reaches the same AP without any door or
electrical feature, which shows the thermal channel alone also encodes the
prescribed event.

Two additional fixture facts restrict interpretation:

1. **The electrical block is run-invariant.** Across 16 development runs and 2715
   compared anchors, every electrical/applied-state column is identical at the same
   anchor time (`mismatch_count = 0`). It cannot encode run, pair or scenario identity
   in this fixture.
2. **The cumulative electrical features are run-age proxies.** `energy_counter_wh`
   and `past_integrated_cooling_energy_wh` are monotonic in time and have a Spearman
   correlation of 1.0 with anchor time. Their univariate label AUC (0.60 / 0.60) is
   time-driven, not scenario-driven; the univariate scenario AUC of every electrical
   feature is exactly 0.5.

Therefore the observed zero delta is consistent with a saturated synthetic control
and an uninformative electrical block. It is **not** evidence that electrical
variables have zero physical value, and it is **not** evidence of feature equivalence.

## 5. Electrical provenance and leakage audit

Full per-feature audit: `A3-electrical-audit-W15.csv` (SHA-256
`db5f7ce69c92246ebe17a7a5d100d478bec830493fbaafb6e3522b428069c6b8`).
Frozen registry text supplies each physical meaning, quality requirement,
timestamp domain and missingness rule; W15 adds the shortcut assessment:

| Feature | Origin | Availability at t | Audit class |
|---|---|---|---|
| `applied_cooling` | POWER_PATH applied state | observed at or before t | controller-action proxy |
| `applied_duty` | POWER_PATH applied duty | observed at or before t | controller-action proxy |
| `time_since_confirmed_applied_change` | past-only applied transition | transition observed at or before t | controller-action proxy |
| `cooling_voltage_v` | PZEM-017 branch voltage | observed at or before t | downstream electrical response |
| `cooling_current_a` | PZEM-017 branch current | observed at or before t | downstream electrical response |
| `cooling_power_w` | PZEM-017 branch power | observed at or before t | downstream response + PWM sampling |
| `energy_counter_wh` | PZEM register with reset refusal | observed at or before t | run-age/scenario identifier risk |
| `past_integrated_cooling_energy_wh` | past-only power integral | all intervals since first VALID sample | run-age identifier + downstream response |

PZEM observes the cooling branch only; none of these features is whole-device
energy, verified actuation or thermal response. The fixture's electrical block is
generated by the same fixture as the thermal trajectory, so its zero delta cannot
be transferred to a real plant.
